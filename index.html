about

From mechanical drawings to digital waveforms

I started in mechanical engineering — B.Tech from VIT, specializing in automotive systems — before moving into VLSI verification. That background shows up in how I work: I still think in terms of tolerances, edge cases, and what happens when a system is pushed outside its intended envelope, just applied to registers and bus transactions instead of parts and assemblies.

Since 2023 I've been building SystemVerilog and UVM testbenches at PRS Semicon Technologies, verifying AMBA protocols (AXI, AHB, APB), MIPI camera and display interfaces (CSI/DSI), and most recently a RISC-V processor core. The common thread across all of it is the same loop: write the environment, drive constrained-random and directed stimulus, watch the scoreboard, and dig into waveforms when it disagrees with the reference model.

I'm looking for design verification roles where I can keep going deeper on protocol-level and processor-level verification, and pick up new architectures along the way.
